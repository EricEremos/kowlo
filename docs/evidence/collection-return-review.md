The headline becomes “A familiar place, a new layer.” The chapter identifies the additional three photo locations and the lower section says “Your story grows here.” This gives an existing district a visible enrichment state without awarding another district. The copy identifies Sheung Wan as a suggested place and preserves the distinction between the place label and original photo GPS.

The seven pieces illustrate a denser composition; they are not extracted photo colours, a final palette algorithm, or a rule requiring one permanent bar per photo. A production composition must remain bounded with large libraries, handle repeated colours and missing thumbnails, and retain a useful non-colour representation. New distinct districts change the district count; returns enrich chapters; repeated scans of the same assets must do neither twice. The initial library scan should present one consolidated discovery summary. These proposed rules supplement `AUTOMATIC_LIBRARY_AMENDMENT.md` and do not approve its native-platform change.

The state reuses the collection's Newsreader/DM Sans styles, bound colour and spacing variables, auto-layout groups, and sample colours. It is an isolated visual comparison: copied navigation reactions were removed because they would lead back to the earlier four-photo sample chapter. The original collection's three outgoing links were preserved. The new state does not simulate a permission grant, scan, or implemented reward transition.
