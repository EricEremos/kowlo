## Design decision

The mark consists of a vertical shore-like stem and two separated arms. Their opening suggests a harbour channel; it does not claim to reproduce a geographic boundary. The K silhouette remains stable while restrained colour expresses the personal atlas concept. The master contains three editable vector paths, with fills bound to the existing Memory print ink, slate and clay variables (115:65, 115:69, 115:68).

The 192 px app-icon application uses the paper-coloured silhouette against harbour ink. Separate 16, 24, 32 and 64 px instances test monochrome recognition. The identity board reuses Space Grotesk for the wordmark, Newsreader for the editorial headline, and DM Sans for supporting text. Auto-layout governs the board's structural groups; no new boxed UI card system was introduced.

The atlas preview preserves the existing geographic memory-print instance and changes the wordmark to KOWLO. The original atlas/prototype and prior naming studies were preserved. Its counts and pigments remain illustrative, not actual user data or a functioning photo-library scan.

## Verification

Figma PNG exports were downloaded and visually inspected at 1440 × 1080 and 390 × 844. The mark remains recognisable in the displayed monochrome size proofs; no cropped text was observed. A structural query confirms eight identity-board text nodes with no text bounding boxes outside the board, three token-bound editable vectors, and instances at exactly 16/24/32/64 px. The atlas preview contains KOWLO and no residual Kongloom wordmark.
