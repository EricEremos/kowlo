# Brand direction: Placefold

Historical first proposal. Kongloom was subsequently proposed and rejected by the user. See the current [naming shortlist](BRAND_NAMING_SHORTLIST.md); no replacement name is approved.

Status: first design proposal for user review, 5 September 2026. External project names are not yet changed.

## Recommendation

**Placefold** combines a place with the act of folding a map or keeping a page. Pronounce it “place-fold”. It supports a compact folded-map symbol and a product that can expand beyond Hong Kong. These are design judgments, not tested marketing results.

Promise: **Keep the places that stay with you.**

Product explanation: A private atlas made from the locations in your photos. Starting in Hong Kong.

The reference app informs the utility of geographic records. This proposal develops its own folded-map mark, editorial typography, paper-and-harbour palette, and local-first photo import story.

| Candidate | Strength | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Placefold | Compact, descriptive association, strong symbol | Compound name needs a short product explanation |
| Somewhere Kept | Emotional and memorable phrase | Longer, less direct app-name recall |
| Foldwhere | Distinctive word shape | Less natural to pronounce and explain |

## Preliminary collision checks

Exact-name web searches, including App Store and Google Play restrictions for Placefold, did not surface an obvious competing app during this research pass. This is limited search evidence, not proof of availability. Domain, social handle and trademark availability remain unverified.

Rejected candidates with relevant existing products: [Tidekeep](https://www.tidekeep.com/) and [Keepscape](https://devpost.com/software/keepscape). Avoid positioning close to an existing memory product simply because a name sounds suitable.

## Design system

Newsreader headings and DM Sans body text; nine shared type styles. Warm paper #F6F4EE, ink #20312B, harbour #275A4A, mist #E5ECE5, muted #606C65, clay #995D40. Semantic variables bind to primitives, with reusable spacing and radii.

The welcome map is abstract brand artwork. Desktop district outlines are derived from official administrative geometry, include marine areas, and are labelled accordingly. They do not imply travelled area.
