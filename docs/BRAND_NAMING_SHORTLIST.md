# Naming shortlist, 6 September 2026

Selection update: the user selected KOWLO and its colour association. [The current identity](KOWLO_IDENTITY.md) supersedes the decision status in this historical shortlist.

Status: KOWLO is the leading direction following the user's positive response to its colour association. Kongloom is rejected. This records a developing brand direction, not commercial clearance or a completed external rename.

Outcome: propose distinctive Hong Kong-rooted names with sound, product philosophy, marketing uses, icon implications and explicit weaknesses. Success is a concrete comparative shortlist, not an unsupported marketing score.

## Selection criteria

Sound and repeatability; Hong Kong association; personal photographic geography; room for a recognisable visual identity; search ambiguity. These are design judgments pending audience testing.

| Name | Intended pronunciation | Philosophy and origin | Brand expression | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KOWLO | KOW-loh; 카울로 | Coined shortening inspired by Kowloon. The familiar city becomes a personal city through accumulated memories. Not a Cantonese translation. | Compact independent wordmark; a K built from two shore-like forms; “Hong Kong, in your colours.” | May imply Kowloon-only coverage; spelling can be mistaken for an incomplete Kowloon. |
| HUEKONG | HYOO-kong; 휴콩 | English hue plus Hong Kong. Photos contribute their colours to a personal atlas. Not a Cantonese expression. | An H divided by a harbour channel; “Your photos. Your colours. Your Hong Kong.” | Still a constructed compound; hue is less familiar to some audiences; could resemble a photo-filter product. |
| WANORA | wah-NOR-ah; 와노라 | Coined brand construction inspired by wan, 灣, bay. The ending is chosen for sound, not claimed to have a Cantonese meaning. Memories gather around a personal shore. | Open shoreline arc; calm lifestyle identity; “A city that becomes yours.” | Hong Kong link needs a descriptor; Wanora is also an Australian locality. |
| HEUNG | Cantonese root 香, hoeng1; requires pronunciation testing | Rooted in the first character of 香港. Interpret memories lingering like a scent as a brand metaphor, not a literal translation. | Contemporary typographic seal; “The city stays with you.” | Pronunciation and spelling friction internationally; fragrance associations and existing uses make discovery harder. |

Recommendation: KOWLO for a compact consumer brand; HUEKONG for immediate connection to the distinctive photo-colour experience. WANORA is a softer alternative; HEUNG is a culturally rooted comparison, not the preferred international launch choice.

The app's central behaviour remains automatic geographic composition from existing photo locations, subject to the unresolved platform permission amendment. Names must not imply continuous movement tracking. Coordinate evidence controls placement; optional image colours influence expression, not geolocation.

## Research and limits

- [Hong Kong Tourism Board neighbourhood guide](https://tastehk.discoverhongkong.com/zh-hk/neighbourhoods) documents Kowloon and the city's differentiated neighbourhoods. KOWLO is our invented adaptation, not an official place name.
- [CantoDict 灣](https://www.cantonese.sheik.co.uk/dictionary/characters/1169/?cf=1&full=true) gives bay/cove and waan1, with Hong Kong place-name examples. [EdUHK research](https://repository.eduhk.hk/en/publications/%E9%A6%99%E6%B8%AF%E5%9C%B0%E5%90%8D%E7%81%A3%E7%9A%84%E7%89%B9%E6%AE%8A%E8%AE%80%E6%B3%95/) describes tonal exceptions; do not claim all Wan place names have identical pronunciation or characters.
- [CantoDict 香](https://www.cantonese.sheik.co.uk/dictionary/characters/%E9%A6%99/?all=true&full=true) records hoeng1, fragrant/aromatic and 香港. HEUNG is a proposed brand spelling, not Jyutping.
- [Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL32985) confirms Wanora is an existing locality.
- [Heung Yau](https://heungyau.com/en/pages/about-heung-yau) is an existing Hong Kong incense brand. HEUNG has cultural authenticity but is not a blank commercial territory.
- Exact-name web queries did not identify an obvious competing app for KOWLO or HUEKONG in returned results. HUEKONG also surfaced as part of a person's name. This limited search does not establish uniqueness, domains, handles or trademark availability.
- TIDRA excluded: an [existing memory tracker](https://apps.apple.com/tz/app/tidra/id6749569243) already uses it. WANSEE excluded due to existing entertainment usage; HUEWAN deprioritised after an exact corporate-name match.

## KOWLO: personal colours direction

User feedback: KOWLO feels a little like “colour”; each person could create their own beautifully coloured Hong Kong. The user reiterated that this association is appealing. Develop KOWLO around this direction rather than generating another unrelated shortlist.

Brand promise: **Hong Kong, in your colours.**

Korean campaign copy: **당신의 색으로 물드는 홍콩.**

Philosophy: one shared city, a different personal composition for everyone. Existing photo locations provide the geographic structure; colours sampled from the user's images provide the expressive material. Accumulation makes the atlas more personal and richer over time.

Name explanation: KOWLO is a coined name inspired by Kowloon. The colour association is the user's subjective sound association and a creative brand opportunity, not an exact English homophone or a Cantonese translation. Citywide coverage must remain clear in onboarding and descriptions.

Visual direction:

- A quiet warm neutral interface lets each person's geographic artwork carry the colour. Avoid assigning every user the same decorative rainbow or neon palette.
- Develop a stable, recognisable K silhouette with a harbour-like negative-space opening. Explore a small number of coloured fragments as the expressive layer; keep the silhouette readable in monochrome and at app-icon sizes.
- Use photo-derived pigments, fine marks and layered geographic shapes rather than a wall of rectangular thumbnails. Preserve recognisable geography and readable map controls.
- New locations reveal evidenced areas; revisits enrich their visual texture. One photograph must not imply that the user explored an entire district. Richer artwork is the reward, with restrained supporting progress information.

Draft product copy: “Watch your Hong Kong take colour.” / “A new shade of your city.” These are creative proposals, not tested conversion claims.

Next smallest design action: develop a KOWLO identity study in the existing Figma file using this promise, a monochrome-first symbol and two restrained applications. Exact/phonetic commercial conflicts and pronunciation remain to be assessed before launch.
