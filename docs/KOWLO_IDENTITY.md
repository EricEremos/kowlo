# KOWLO

**Hong Kong, in your colours.**

Current identity, 6 September 2026. The user selected the KOWLO direction and its association with colour. This supersedes the earlier Placefold and Kongloom proposals. The GitHub portfolio uses `kowlo` as its repository name.

KOWLO is a coined name inspired by Kowloon. The sound can suggest “colour”; that association is creative wordplay, not a Cantonese translation or a claim about the word's linguistic origin. The product covers Hong Kong, not only Kowloon.

## The promise

Your photographs give Hong Kong its own colours. The places you return to gather into a personal atlas: familiar geography, expressed through your memories.

Primary line: **Hong Kong, in your colours.**

Descriptor: **Your private Hong Kong photo atlas.**

## Visual expression

- A clear KOWLO wordmark, generous spacing and editorial typography.
- Warm paper `#F6F4EF`, harbour ink `#263B3E` and secondary ink `#566969` keep essential text stable and readable.
- Clay, slate, lavender, ochre and forest accents give the illustrative atlas variety. In the intended product, optional colours derived locally from saved photographs personalise the artwork.
- Geographic marks accumulate into a city, with district chapters bringing individual memories closer.

The user endorsed the colour association and personal-colour direction. This does not establish that every decorative swatch has been individually approved. Screens and portfolio artwork still contain illustrative photographs, palettes and totals.

Coordinates determine location. Colour affects expression, never the validity of a visit or earned district progress. Repeated visits deepen a chapter without increasing the distinct district count.
