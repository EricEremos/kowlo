# KOWLO

## Register

product

## Users

People rediscovering Hong Kong through photographs they already own, on phones and desktop.

## Product Purpose

Turn located photographs into a private geographic journal. The primary experience is an accumulating atlas, followed by place chapters and memories. Automatic phone-library enumeration depends on the pending native-platform amendment.

## Brand Personality

Personal, artistic, rewarding. KOWLO is the selected direction: Hong Kong, in your colours. The name is inspired by Kowloon; its association with colour is creative wordplay. See docs/KOWLO_IDENTITY.md for the current identity and boundaries.

## Anti-references

Avoid a copy of Footprints, generic thumbnail pins as the dominant map language, repetitive cards, and manual Add places chores. Footprints informs geographic accumulation; folded marks provide a distinct expression.

## Design Principles

- Geography carries the primary story; photographs enrich its chapters.
- Existing memories fill the atlas; revisits enrich places without overstating coverage.
- Use alignment, whitespace and typography before visible enclosure.
- Preserve source GPS and disclose inferred labels and illustrative content.
- Keep optional photo-colour analysis separate from metadata-only mapping.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The existing design contract targets readable contrast, generous touch bounds, non-colour cues, reduced motion and visible focus. Static Figma verification does not establish runtime WCAG conformance. See docs/ATLAS_DESIGN_REVISION.md for current tokens, evidence and limitations.
