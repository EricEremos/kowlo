## Geographic data

**Overview coastline:** Made with Natural Earth. Its vector and raster data are [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). The [source geometry](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_land.geojson) is clipped and stylised for the memory-print artwork; it is not a venue-accuracy map.

**Hong Kong administrative boundaries:** Source: Home Affairs Department, Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, distributed through [DATA.GOV.HK](https://data.gov.hk/). The government and relevant organisations retain ownership of their intellectual property. [Original boundary dataset](https://www.had.gov.hk/psi/hong-kong-administrative-boundaries/hksar_18_district_boundary.json) · [Terms of use](https://data.gov.hk/en/terms-and-conditions) · [Pinned provenance](data/reference/hk-districts.provenance.json).

**Place points and names:** Source: Lands Department, Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, via the **Common Spatial Data Infrastructure (CSDI) Portal**. Intellectual property in the source data remains with the Government and relevant organisations. [Source service](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/server/rest/services/common/landsd_rcd_1648571595120_89752/FeatureServer) · [CSDI terms](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/doc/TNC) · [Pinned provenance](data/reference/hk-places.provenance.json).

Government reference data are provided as-is. Inclusion does not imply endorsement of KOWLO. Derived suggestions and decorative artwork are project interpretations; preserve these source acknowledgements when redistributing the reference data or derived copies.

## Typography and software

Designs and the working web preview use Newsreader, DM Sans and Space Grotesk. The preview serves the fonts locally. Pinned source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in [the font manifest](app/fonts/sources.json); each font's SIL Open Font License is included beside its binary in [app/fonts](app/fonts). Software dependency licenses remain with their respective authors; the installed dependency metadata identifies those terms.

A repository-wide software license has not been selected. Public availability of this portfolio should not be read as a separate grant of reuse rights. Third-party asset and data terms apply independently.
